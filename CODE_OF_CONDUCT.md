# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous project. Participants should be able to contribute without harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific, especially when reviewing defects or evidence.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Represent test, deployment, and production results accurately.
- Protect security reports, credentials, customer information, and personal data.
- Welcome questions and help contributors understand project conventions.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that affect technical or release decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of confidential material, sustained disruption, and knowingly fabricated technical evidence are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Do not open a public issue when doing so would expose a target or sensitive information. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, restrict participation, or ban participants. Enforcement decisions should be documented privately and made without retaliation.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project in related public or private forums.
